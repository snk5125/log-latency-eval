Heads-up

    Peak = steady state: standing data stops growing once the longest retention has elapsed and daily expiry equals daily ingest. Cost = storage (per class, decimal GB) + PUT + lifecycle-transition requests. Excludes retrievals/restores, GET traffic, KMS, replication, and egress. Standard is tranche-priced on the aggregate Standard footprint across all tiers (first 50 TB / next 450 TB / over 500 TB — boundaries fixed, rates editable), and the no-lifecycle baseline uses the same schedule; per-tier rows allocate Standard at the blended rate. Tranches reset monthly per account and region, so any other Standard usage in the account would shift the blend slightly. All bytes are as stored — apply your compression ratio before entering ingest. Minimum-duration charges (IA 30d, Glacier Instant/Flexible 90d, Deep Archive 180d) are flagged but not added to the bill.
